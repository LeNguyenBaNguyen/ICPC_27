# Third-party material and licensing scope

The artifact contains code excerpts and historical trajectories from fourteen public Python projects. Source repository names, revision pins and function locations are retained in `artifact/src/common.py`, `artifact/src/redraw_experiment.py` and the datasets. Upstream notices are collected under `artifact/third_party_licenses/`; `sources.json` records pinned retrieval references and hashes. These notices retain their original authors and terms. Do not remove attribution to anonymize the research authors.

Figure icons are Twemoji graphics (Twitter and contributors), used under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Source SVGs and converted PDF assets are in `manuscript/figures/icons/`. The original graphics license is included with the upstream notices; the manuscript caption also credits Twemoji.

Model weights are not distributed. Experimental outputs and runtime fingerprints identify the models used, including Qwen2.5-Coder and Llama 3.1; model notices embedded in recorded runtime metadata are retained.

No project-wide license for the new research code was present in the supplied snapshot. This packaging step does not assign a license on behalf of the authors. Existing third-party notices continue to apply to their respective material.
